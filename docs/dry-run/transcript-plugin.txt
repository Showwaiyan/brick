===== USER 7 =====
What would you do about the first gap? Give me your suggestion.
----- CLAUDE -----
My suggestion: check the rate limit *before* comparing the password hash. Reject immediately if the limit's already hit, without touching the hash at all. Reasoning — hashing is deliberately expensive (that's the point, to slow down brute force), so doing it on an attempt you're going to reject anyway wastes that cost for nothing; checking the limit first is cheap and short-circuits the expensive step.

That's just my take — if you agree, tell me in your own words and I'll record it, then you can resolve the gap yourself with `/brick:resolve g1 --note "..."`.
